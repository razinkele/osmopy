previous report, `baltic_ices_validation_2026-04-18.md`, is kept as history; its hand-written
findings describe the April configuration, which has since been recalibrated (percid removals,
cod_east M, herring/sprat/flounder F), so its numbers no longer apply.

### What changed with the split

- **cod_west** (`fsh0` → `cod.27.22-24`, tonnes): F 0.039 vs ICES 0.912 (ratio 0.04, allowlisted);
  biomass target [4 000, 25 000] t **overlaps** the ICES SSB envelope [5 303, 12 896] t — the
  April `B_KNOWN_EXCEPTIONS` entry for aggregate cod is therefore retired.
- **cod_east** (`fsh8` → `cod.27.24-32`, index-unit): F 0.010 vs the index-weighted ICES F 0.164
  (ratio 0.06, allowlisted: the stock has been under a targeted-fishing closure since 2019,
  ICES F fell to ~0.03 by 2021-2022); no biomass envelope (index-unit, excluded as before).
- Recruitment: `cod_west` now gets a real ICES comparison (age 1, counts); `cod_east` is skipped
  via the new `recruitment_index_stocks` manifest list — its R is on the same relative scale as
  its SSB, which the SSB unit flag alone did not tell us (`her.27.25-2932` has an index SSB but
  absolute R).

### Soft-tolerance drift hits (validator exit 1; NOT allowlisted in the test)

- **herring (F ✗):** model 0.380 vs ICES 0.208 — 1.83×, outside the script's [0.5×, 1.5×] band,
  inside the test's [0.25×, 4×] severe fence. In April the model sat at 0.150 (0.72×).
- **sprat (F ✗):** model 0.164 vs ICES 0.369 — 0.44×. In April 0.320 (0.87×).
- **flounder (F ✗, allowlisted):** model 1.368 vs ICES 0.215 — **6.4× above**, the opposite sign
  of April (0.040, 0.19×). The calibrated base F is a stability requirement incommensurable with
  the ICES anchor (`baltic_f_hindcast_2026-08-23.md`, decision 5); the allowlist rationale is
  corrected accordingly.

Whether the herring/sprat drift is real or the soft band is stale is a calibration decision, not
a validator one: both moved as part of the 2026-07/08 recalibrations recorded in
`docs/baltic_percid_removals_outcome_2026-07-28.md` and the F hindcast work.
