### What the refresh changed

- **Assessments.** Nine stocks moved to their 2026 SAG assessment. `cod.27.22-24` stays on 2022,
  the last with absolute SSB and F (2023 is relative, 2025 a harvest-rate ratio), and
  `fle.27.2223` on 2024 (no 2025 or 2026 entry). The window is now **2020–2024**.
- **cod_west:** ICES F 0.912 → 0.902; SSB envelope [5 303, 12 896] → [5 303, 9 133] t, still
  overlapping. Its snapshot ends at 2021–22, so the new window covers **two years**, not five; the
  validator warns about it. Nothing better exists until ICES resumes an absolute assessment.
- **cod_east:** the 2026 assessment publishes **no F** (2024: a relative index, 0.164), so the row
  now has no ICES F. It was already without a verdict.
- **herring:** ICES F 0.208 → 0.166, still without a verdict (partial coverage); SSB envelope
  [727 425, 812 287] → [781 431, 812 526] t, overlapping. `her.27.25-2932`'s F is now labelled
  `F/Fmsy` (it was `F`, units `ratio`); it was already excluded as index-unit.
- **sprat:** ICES F 0.369 → 0.377, model 0.369, **in tolerance**. SSB envelope [892 276,
  1 126 771] → [634 913, 1 121 262] t, still overlapping, but **ICES SSB fell to 0.63 Mt in 2024
  and 0.62 Mt in 2025**, below the 0.8 Mt floor of the model's sprat biomass target. The overlap
  is carried by 2020–2022. Whether to lower the target floor is a calibration decision, not
  taken here. (The 2026-07-28 re-check's "sprat 0.90–1.13 Mt" predates this assessment.)
- **flounder:** relative F 0.356 → 0.372. `bwp.27.2729-32` now publishes a length-based
  `Lfm/Lmean` ratio in SAG's F column, so the relative figure averages F/FMSY with a length
  indicator. It is display-only (no verdict); read it as such. `bzq.27.2425` moved from a tonnes/h
  index to a 2026 entry labelled "SSB, tonnes" that reads 113–207 against 2.7–20 kt of catch, so
  `scripts/_pull_ices_snapshots.py` overrides its unit to index (`UNIT_OVERRIDES`).
- **Catch targets** (`biomass_targets.csv`, window still 2018–2022) moved only through SAG's
  revisions of past years: herring −53 t of 302 512 (−0.018%), cod_east +0.17 t, flounder −0.03 t;
  cod_west and sprat unchanged.
